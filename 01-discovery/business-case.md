# Business Case – NovaMart Order Fulfillment Improvement

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** October 2025  
**Author:** Business Analyst  
**Sponsor:** Marcus Lee (COO)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

NovaMart is losing an estimated **$1.8M annually** due to delayed shipments, stock-outs, and high return rates caused by poor inventory visibility and manual fulfillment processes.  

This project proposes implementing a modern Inventory & Order Management solution combined with process redesign. Expected benefits include:

- 35–45% reduction in average order cycle time  
- 25% reduction in stock-outs  
- 15–20% decrease in returns related to wrong/late items  
- Improved Net Promoter Score (NPS) by 12–15 points  

**Recommended Investment:** $420,000 (Year 1)  
**Payback Period:** 14–16 months  
**3-Year ROI:** ~210%

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Current state issues:
- Average order-to-ship time: **4.8 days** (industry benchmark: 1.5–2.5 days)
- Stock-out rate on top 200 SKUs: **18%**
- Order accuracy: **91%** (target > 98%)
- High manual effort in warehouse (paper-based picking lists)
- No real-time inventory visibility between online and offline stores

Impact:
- Customer complaints up 34% YoY
- Increased expedited shipping costs
- Lost sales from stock-outs estimated at $950k/year

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

1. Implement cloud-based Inventory Management System (IMS) integrated with existing e-commerce platform and POS.
2. Redesign warehouse processes (zone picking + barcode scanning).
3. Introduce real-time inventory synchronization.
4. Create exception handling dashboards for Customer Service and Warehouse managers.

---

## 4. Options Considered

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons | Recommendation |
|--------|-------------|------|------|----------------|
| A | Do Nothing | No cost | Continued losses | Rejected |
| B | Process improvement only (no new system) | Low cost | Limited impact | Rejected |
| C | Full IMS + Process Redesign | High impact, scalable | Higher investment | **Recommended** |
| D | Custom-built system | Full control | High risk, longer timeline | Rejected |

---

## 5. High-Level Cost-Benefit

**Costs (Year 1):**
- Software license & implementation: $280,000
- Training & change management: $65,000
- Process consulting & BA effort: $45,000
- Contingency (10%): $30,000  
**Total:** ≈ $420,000

**Benefits (Annual, starting Year 2):**
- Reduced stock-outs & lost sales: $380,000
- Lower shipping & return costs: $210,000
- Labor efficiency gains: $145,000  
**Total Annual Benefit:** ≈ $735,000

---

## 6. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Low user adoption | Medium | High | Strong change management + super-user program |
| Integration issues with legacy POS | Medium | High | Early technical spike + vendor assessment |
| Scope creep | High | Medium | Strict change control + MoSCoW prioritization |

---

## 7. Recommendation

Proceed with Option C. Form a cross-functional project team and begin detailed requirements and vendor evaluation immediately.
