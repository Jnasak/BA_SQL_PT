# MoSCoW Prioritization

| Priority | Item | Rationale |
|----------|------|-----------|
| **Must Have** | Real-time multi-location inventory | Core to all other improvements |
| **Must Have** | Automatic order allocation engine | Removes biggest bottleneck |
| **Must Have** | Mobile scanning for picking & receiving | Required for accuracy and speed |
| **Must Have** | Integration with e-commerce platform & POS | Single source of truth |
| **Should Have** | Zone-optimized picking lists | Significant productivity gain |
| **Should Have** | Low-stock alerts & suggested POs | Prevents future stock-outs |
| **Should Have** | Exception / delayed order dashboard | Improves customer experience |
| **Could Have** | Advanced analytics & forecasting | Valuable but not required for MVP |
| **Could Have** | Supplier portal | Future phase |
| **Won't Have (this phase)** | Full ERP replacement | Out of scope and too large |
| **Won't Have (this phase)** | AI demand sensing | Future opportunity |
